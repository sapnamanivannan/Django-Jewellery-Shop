Stories in gold — handcrafted jewellery, made to last

At ShineJewels we blend traditional craftsmanship with contemporary design. Each piece is designed to become a part of your story — heirlooms for the everyday.

Founded in 2012, our studio sources responsibly mined metals and ethically certified stones. We create engagement rings, everyday necklaces, and custom pieces that celebrate life’s moments.

Why ShineJewels?

Master craftsmanship

Every piece is hand-finished by experienced artisans — attention to detail is our promise.

Ethical sourcing

We use responsibly sourced metals and certified gemstones; transparency matters to us.

Lifetime care

Free cleaning & inspection for life — because jewellery should be worn, not stored away.

Our story

What began as a small family workshop has grown into a studio known for subtle, elegant designs. We combine classic techniques with modern silhouettes to create pieces that are comfortable to wear daily and beautiful enough for special occasions.
